Support

A few pointers to help you get back to reading.

Look up a word

Point your iPhone's rear camera at printed English text, center a word under the reticle, and tap the camera view. Use good light and keep the page flat. If the camera cannot focus, move the phone a little farther from the page.

You can also type a word in the search field. Text lookup works without camera permission.

Allow camera access

If you previously denied access, open iOS Settings, find WordLens in the app settings, and enable Camera. If the camera is unavailable, you can continue using text lookup.

Offline definitions

Definitions and suggestions use the English dictionary bundled with the app. An internet connection is not required. If a word is missing, check the spelling or try its base form. Some words may not be included.

If the dictionary is unavailable, first try updating the app. Reinstalling is another option, but deleting the app removes its local history and settings.

Pronunciation

Use the pronunciation button beside a word to hear it with Apple's system speech. If you cannot hear it, check your device volume and audio output.

Clear lookup history

Open History, choose Clear, and confirm Clear History. This removes the history stored by the app on this device. Copies in device backups are managed separately through your Apple settings.

Contact Heavyside

Report a problem or ask a privacy question through the WordLens GitHub issue tracker. A GitHub account is required to submit an issue. For a bug report, include your iOS version, app version, and steps to reproduce the problem.

Issues are public. Do not include private or sensitive information, camera images, or lookup history.

Read the WordLens privacy policy for details about data handling.
