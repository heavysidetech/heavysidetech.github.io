WordLens by Heavyside

Privacy policy

Effective November 25, 2026

WordLens processes camera images and dictionary lookups on your iPhone. Heavyside does not collect your images, recognized text, or lookup history.

This policy covers the WordLens iOS app and its privacy and support website, provided by Heavyside.

Camera and text recognition

WordLens accesses your camera only with your permission to recognize printed words. Camera frames and text recognition are processed on your device using Apple's Vision framework. Frames are held temporarily in memory while you use the camera feature; the app does not upload them, save them to your photo library, or persist them as image files.

You can type words to look them up without granting camera permission. You can revoke camera access at any time in iOS Settings.

Dictionary lookups and pronunciation

Definitions and word suggestions come from a dictionary bundled with the app and work offline. Typed and recognized words are not sent to a dictionary service. Pronunciation uses Apple's system speech synthesizer; WordLens does not record your voice or request microphone access.

Data stored on your device

WordLens stores successful lookups locally, including the word, definition, part of speech when available, and lookup time. It also stores your default camera zoom preference. History remains until you clear it or delete the app; the zoom preference remains until you change it or delete the app.

The app does not synchronize history through CloudKit or a Heavyside server. App data may be included in device or iCloud backups according to your Apple settings. Those backups are managed separately from WordLens.

Your choices and deletion

To delete lookup history, open History, choose Clear, and confirm Clear History. You can change the default camera zoom in the app's settings. Deleting the app removes its local data; offloading the app can preserve data. Clearing history or deleting the app does not delete copies in existing backups. Manage backups through your device or Apple account settings.

Heavyside has no server-side copy of your camera images or lookup history to retrieve or delete.

Collection, sharing, and tracking

WordLens has no accounts, advertising, tracking, analytics SDKs, or developer-operated data collection backend. Heavyside does not collect, sell, or share personal information from your use of the app. The app does not include a third-party crash-reporting service. iOS diagnostics and device services are governed by your Apple settings and Apple's policies.

Website visits, external links, and support

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages. We do not add analytics, advertising, tracking scripts, or cookies. GitHub logs visitors' IP addresses for security purposes, as described in GitHub's privacy statement. Opening support or attribution links takes you to external websites subject to their own privacy policies.

If you voluntarily submit a support or privacy question through our GitHub issue tracker, Heavyside can see and use your submission and public GitHub profile information to respond. Issues are public and may remain available until removed through GitHub's controls. Do not include personal, private, or sensitive information, camera images, or lookup history. You can request removal of information you submitted through the same tracker; GitHub controls its own records and retention.

Changes to this policy

We will publish updates on this page and revise the effective date when this policy changes.

Contact

For questions about WordLens or this policy, contact Heavyside through the WordLens issue tracker. For app instructions, visit WordLens support.
